About ChessLeo Academy

Chess strengthens logical thinking, concentration, and patience in young children. Yet traditional chess programs often confront elementary school beginners with steep text barriers, drab interface designs, or aggressive freemium monetization models that push in-app purchases and distracting advertising into children's bedrooms.

ChessLeo Academy was created by Munich-based indie developer Tobias Schudy as an antithesis: a calm, child-centered chess learning environment with clear pedagogy, hand-crafted visuals, and voice-guided instruction. Coach Bird reads every lesson aloud, allowing early readers in primary school to learn at their own pace without frustration.

Rather than dry opening theory, children experience chess as an exciting journey across five school grades—culminating in a personal, printable Master Diploma upon passing the Master Exam.

🎙️
Coach Bird Reads Aloud
Every lesson is narrated by Coach Bird. Early elementary school beginners can learn independently without needing adult help to read lengthy instructions.
🏆
5 Grades & Master Exam
From piece movements and castling to knight forks, pins, skewers, and 7 classic checkmating patterns. Successful students earn a printable Master Diploma.
🎮
27 Minigames & 6,300+ Puzzles
Engaging challenges like Taktik-Detektive, Feld-Ninja, Bauern-Krieg, and Springer-Parcours reinforce chess skills through playful practice.
🛡️
100 % Ad-Free & Offline
Zero advertisements, zero subscriptions, zero in-app purchases. Lessons, minigames, and computer matches work completely without an internet connection.

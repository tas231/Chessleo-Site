The True Origin Story Behind ChessLeo Academy

"The true catalyst for ChessLeo Academy was my own son." — Tobias Schudy, Creator & Indie Developer

It all started during a family vacation. My son stumbled upon a chess set—and was instantly fascinated. He eagerly wanted to learn the rules so he could play matches with his friends. But when the kids tried out the large, well-known chess apps, disappointment set in quickly: for elementary-school beginners, the interfaces were overwhelming and dense with text. Instead of patient, child-friendly teaching, they were greeted by costly subscriptions, constant advertising interruptions, and artificial pressure—from losing daily experience streaks to mindless clicking through mini-drills just to collect virtual badges, without ever truly understanding the board.

That was the turning point. As a father, I decided to build a chess school from scratch, designed purely through the eyes of a child: with a friendly mentor (Coach Bird) who reads every lesson aloud, a structured curriculum across five school grades, and a painted Magic Table home screen that follows the real time of day, the seasons and festivals. My son became my chief tester and most honest critic—he embraced the journey with such enthusiasm that he could hardly wait each day until I finished coding the next lesson in the evening.

The most rewarding moment for me as a father arrived recently: for his birthday, he didn't ask for video games, but for a real, handcrafted wooden chess set. Today, we sit together at the table playing real matches. And every now and then, he still opens ChessLeo to brush up on tactics—because as he puts it: "When Coach Bird repeats it, it sticks so much better in my memory." My heartfelt hope is that many children discover this exact same joy: learning the fundamentals on their screen, but then bringing that love into the real world—playing with friends, siblings, and grandparents on a real wooden board, and maybe even joining a chess club to become a true master.

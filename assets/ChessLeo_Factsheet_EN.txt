TARGET AUDIENCE & PEDAGOGICAL CONCEPT
-------------------------------------
Target Audience:    for elementary-school beginners (thanks to spoken explanations by Coach Bird, also ideal for early readers)
Coach & Mentor:     Coach Bird reads every lesson aloud – also for kids who cannot yet read fluently
Curriculum:         Prologue 'What is Chess?' (6 interactive pages) plus 5 progressive grades:
                    • Grade 1: The chessboard, movement of all pieces from pawn to queen, en passant (ninja special rule)
                    • Grade 2: Piece values, golden opening rules (center, develop pieces, castling), and first tactics (knight fork, pin, skewer)
                    • Grade 3: First checkmate patterns (ladder mate, kiss of death), recognizing and defending scholar's mate, Italian game
                    • Grade 4: 7 master checkmate patterns (back rank mate, smothered mate, swallow's tail, etc.) and the master exam with Coach Bird
                    • Grade 5: Endgames (opposition, key squares, fortress, skewer in rook endgames) – unlocks after passing the master exam
Graduation:         One personal printable Master Diploma upon passing the master exam at the end of Grade 4 (printable via AirPrint)

FOUNDER STORY & BACKGROUND
--------------------------
"The true catalyst for ChessLeo Academy was my own son."
It all started during a family vacation: my son discovered chess and eagerly wanted
to learn the rules to play matches with his friends. However, existing apps overwhelmed
elementary-school beginners with text barriers, subscription traps, advertising interruptions,
and artificial pressure from losing streaks.
As a father, I created ChessLeo Academy: 100% ad-free, no subscriptions, featuring
voice narration by Coach Bird and a structured 5-grade curriculum.
The most rewarding result: for his birthday, my son asked for a real wooden chess set—
today, we sit together and play matches on a real board!

TARGET AUDIENCE & PEDAGOGICAL CONCEPT
-------------------------------------
Target Audience: Elementary-school beginners (ages around early school years)
Spoken Mentor:   "Coach Bird" reads every lesson aloud (ideal for young beginners who cannot yet read fluently)
Curriculum:      5 structured school grades (Prologue + Grades 1 to 5)
                 From piece movements and special rules (castling, en passant) to tactics
                 (forks, pins, skewers) and 7 master checkmate patterns.
Graduation:      Personal printable Master Diploma upon passing the master exam.
Mini-Games:      27 interactive mini-games & 6,300+ puzzles:
                 Tactics Detectives, Rook Coin Rush, Board Ninja, Stalemate Trap,
                 Pawn Wars, and Knight Parkour.
Design Worlds:   3 selectable visual worlds (Classic Elegant, Vibrant Kids, Art & Culture).

SAFETY & CHILD PRIVACY
----------------------
Advertising:     100% ad-free
Data Privacy:    Zero child data collection, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, no IDFA
Online Chat:     No online chat with strangers, no social media feeds
Offline:         Lessons, mini-games, and computer matches work without internet
                 (ideal for road trips, flights, and school commutes).
